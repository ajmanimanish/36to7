Cultural Roots, Modern Lens

Respecting tradition by translating it into real life.

In traditional Indian marriage compatibility, 36 Guna across 8 Kootas were checked in an astrological chart before families agreed to a match.

36to7 preserves that 36-point structure while explicitly presenting the modern meanings as our interpretation—translated into practical areas of life that two modern adults can understand, discuss, and experience together.

"36to7 keeps the traditional structure and reinterprets its meaning for modern relationships. The modern lenses are our own interpretation; they are not presented as the original ancient astrological meanings of the Guna."

Product Philosophy

Built around real life, not artificial app engagement.

The user provides the life. 36to7 provides the quiet structure to help you understand what matters.

Private by default. Never a shared score.

Your reflections stay yours. There is no percentage score claiming to tell you whether to marry someone.

Uncertainty is legitimate.

It is normal to say "I don't know yet" or "we haven't discussed this." 36to7 never turns uncertainty into failure.

AI organizes, never judges.

Our AI distinguishes what you said, what you believe about them, and what was explicitly stated. It never invents their internal state.

Explicit partner controls.

If you invite a partner later, both of you maintain independent private spaces. Nothing is shared without your conscious step.
